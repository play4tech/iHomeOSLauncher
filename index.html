Questions you might have.

Which Android devices and versions are supported?

iHomeOS supports Android 13 and later. It is engineered and tested on Samsung One UI, Realme UI, Xiaomi HyperOS, OnePlus OxygenOS, Google Pixel devices, and standard AOSP distributions without requiring root or custom ROMs.

Where do ads appear?

Never on your home screen, desktop pages, wallpaper backdrop, dock, or folders. Small non-intrusive banners can appear only inside secondary menus (like settings), and purchasing iHomeOS Pro removes all advertisements permanently. The launcher strictly conforms with Google Play’s Better Ads and Disruptive Ads policies.

What data does iHomeOS collect?

Zero personal telemetry. iHomeOS does not upload your app list, usage history, photos, or contacts to any external servers. All App Library categorization occurs 100% on-device. Please review our Privacy Policy for full compliance details.

How does the Liquid Glass refraction effect work?

On devices with modern GPU rendering, iHomeOS calculates real-time optical light refraction and chromatic aberration against the wallpaper backdrop. A high-efficiency Gaussian blur fallback runs seamlessly on battery-saver mode or older chipsets.

  1. Liquid Glass optical refraction is calculated in real time using native GPU acceleration. High-performance Gaussian blur fallback is provided on all devices.
  2. Requires Android 13 (API level 33) or later. Some custom manufacturer gesture systems may require setting iHomeOS as the default home app in system settings.
  3. iHomeOS Pro is a single lifetime purchase tied to your Google Play account. It restores automatically on new devices without recurring subscription charges.
  4. iHomeOS is an independent software application developed for the Android platform. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Apple Inc.
