Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

1. Overview

iHomeOS Launcher ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data, application permissions, and third-party advertising services.

2. Data Collection and Usage

iHomeOS Launcher does not collect, harvest, sell, or store any personal information, location data, browsing activity, or contacts on remote servers. All launcher configurations, app categorization tags, widget preferences, and wallpaper blur environments are stored locally on your device.

3. Device Permissions

iHomeOS Launcher requests standard Android launcher permissions strictly to deliver core home screen functionality:

  • Query All Packages: Required to discover and display installed applications on your desktop and App Library.
  • Set Wallpaper: Enables you to customize and preview wallpapers directly from the launcher.
  • Notification Listener: Used exclusively to render unread notification count badges on app icons.

4. Advertising and Analytics

To support ongoing development while offering free access, the launcher displays advertisements via Google AdMob in designated non-disruptive containers (such as the expanded App Library category and settings screens). We also integrate the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain and manage consent for users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom.

  • For more information on how Google uses data in advertising, visit Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.
  • Users who purchase the optional Lifetime Pro in-app purchase immediately enjoy an entirely ad-free experience, and all ad network communication is disabled.

5. In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are handled securely and directly through the official Google Play Billing service. We do not process or retain any payment, credit card, or banking details.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or your privacy when using iHomeOS Launcher, please contact the developer via Google Play Store developer support or by email at support@ihomeos.com.
